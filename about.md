### The Project
The Video Project Suite is a project management application focused on small business video production owners or managers to help navigate running a business and multiple video projects, deliverables and clients.  It grew out of my desire to build software applications to help small business owners as well as meeting with friends who have such video production companies.

### About Me
My name is Nathan Harris and I am finishing up a 2nd Bachelors degree in Computer Science at CU Boulder.  My first degree and prior background is in film production which gives me considerable insight to the domain of users I intend to serve with this project.

### Why Blog at all?
"What is measured is managed."  In addition to being a requirement for my professional development course, I am hoping to learn by documenting my progress as well as use this space to communicate and gain feedback from peers and potential users.
