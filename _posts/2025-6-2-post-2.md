#### **What did you do last week?**
This past week I focused on finalizing my [project proposal]({{ site.baseurl }}/proposal) and narrowing the scope and tech stack. I explored multiple backend frameworks, courses, and tutorials while weighing the trade-offs between building a full stack application via the [Full Stack Open](https://fullstackopen.com/en/) curriculum versus focusing on a more minimal, backend-only API using an enterprise-grade framework. Since my goals and interests aligned most with backend, I decided to focus my time there for this course.

#### **What do you plan to do this week?**
This week, I plan to gather and integrate feedback on my proposal from peers and instructors. I’ll also begin working through one of [Microsoft’s C# tutorials](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/training/paths/get-started-c-sharp-part-1/) and continue developing my project planning documentation.

#### **Are there any impediments in your way?**
My biggest challenge is balancing limited time with deep, high-quality learning. I’m testing the idea that structured tutorials and applied practice will be more effective than diving into a time-consuming traditional course with lectures and reading. This week will be a good test of that approach.

#### **Reflection on the process and improvements**
This past week was difficult in that it required making some hard decisions about my priorities and project scope. I had originally hoped to complete a full stack application, but ultimately decided to focus on learning a new backend framework to strengthen my professional skill set and build toward future iterations of the project while putting additional features and a full functioning front end outside the scope of this course.
